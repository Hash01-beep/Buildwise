🦺 Safety & Risk Assessment

Use the hierarchy of controls rather than relying on PPE alone.


1. Identify

Activity → hazard → people exposed → existing controls.

2. Assess

Estimate likelihood and severity, then determine the risk level.

3. Control

Eliminate → substitute → engineer → administrative controls → PPE.

📚 How to Approach a Construction Process

A generic workflow that can be adapted to different construction activities.

🏗 Before Starting Work

1
Understand drawings, specifications and scope.
2
Check site conditions, access and existing services.
3
Identify hazards and prepare controls.
4
Confirm materials, manpower, tools and sequence.

🔍 During Work

1
Follow the approved method and sequence.
2
Inspect dimensions, levels and workmanship.
3
Record changes, issues and material usage.
4
Stop unsafe work and correct defects.

🏠 Property Buying & Inspection

Price and appearance are only part of a property decision. Check technical, documentary and environmental factors.

📜

Documents

Verify ownership, approvals, applicable restrictions and relevant records with the appropriate authorities/professionals.

🏗️

Structure

Look for significant cracks, settlement signs, corrosion, water damage and undocumented structural changes.

💧

Water & Dampness

Inspect roof drainage, plumbing leaks, damp walls, waterproofing and standing water.

⚡

Utilities

Check visible electrical, water, gas and other service installations for obvious defects.

🌧️

Environment

Consider drainage, flood exposure, heat, erosion, pollution, noise and surrounding development.

💰

Lifecycle Cost

Consider repairs, maintenance, utilities, renovation and replacement costs—not only purchase price.

🧰 Material Selection Guide

Choosing materials is not only about the lowest price.

🧱 Cement / Concrete

Consider specified strength, exposure, mix requirements, storage, batching and curing.

🔩 Reinforcement Steel

Check specified grade, diameter, markings, condition, storage and approved documentation.

🧱 Bricks / Blocks

Consider dimensions, strength, absorption, consistency, defects and intended application.

🎨 Paint

Choose according to surface, exposure, moisture, substrate preparation and required finish.

💧 Waterproofing

First identify the water source; product selection without source diagnosis can fail.

⬛ Tiles

Consider location, water exposure, slip resistance, wear, dimensions and installation system.
